### ✨ What we care about

- **No Administrator permission**: Wumi only asks for what it needs.
- **Your data stays yours**: remove Wumi and everything about your server is erased.
- **Free and open source**: under the MIT license.
- **Bilingual**: every message follows your server's language, 🇬🇧 English or 🇫🇷 French.
